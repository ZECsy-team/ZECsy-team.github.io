The big picture

Four easy moves

1

Get any crypto from a cryptocurrency bank/currency exchange account. Start with USDC, BTC, ETH, or SOL.

2

Swap inside Zodl with its built-in currency exchange.

3

Receive shielded ZEC in your own wallet. Your money is now digital cash: secret, invisible, discreet.

4

Spend secretly on anything by sending to anyone with a Zcash address, with an optional private note.

No separate swap site needed. The swap happens in your wallet app, which keeps things simple and helps avoid mistakes.
Step 1

Get some crypto

Pick whatever is available. Many different assets can become shielded ZEC, so you don’t need ZEC to start.

  1. Buy USDC, BTC (Bitcoin), ETH (Ethereum), or SOL (Solana) in an app you already trust. Violet's cryptocurrency exchange directory lists reputable options.
  2. Start with a small test amount while you learn. $20 to $50 is enough; consider this your "tuition payment."
  3. You may need to show ID or wait for your purchase to clear before you can withdraw to an outside address. See Violet's crypto on-ramping reference sheet for more guidance.
Heads up: buying crypto involves ID checks and variable fees. "Stablecoins" like USDC or USDT are handy because their value won't jump around while you wait to be allowed to make a withdrawal.
Step 2

Set up Zodl

  1. Download Zodl from the link on ZODL.com. Don’t trust links from DMs or ads.
  2. Create a new wallet by opening the app for the first time on your phone.
  3. Your wallet uses shielded addresses by default, so you’re private from day one.
You might be nudged to enable Tor and currency conversion, which are both one-click operations that make you even more discreet and help you know how much local currency your ZEC is worth. Read Violet's "discretion-maxxing" guide for more details.
Step 3

Swap into shielded ZEC

  1. In Zodl, tap Swap from its main screen.
  2. Choose what and where you're swapping from ("USDC on Ethereum" or BTC) and enter the amount.
  3. Enter a refund address: the "receive" address in the app you're sending from. This ensures funds return to you if swapping into ZEC fails.
  4. Review the swap quote, then confirm.
  5. Zodl shows a deposit address and QR code.
  6. From your other app or exchange account, send the exact amount to the swap deposit address.
Double-check the network and amount before sending. A wrong network can mean lost funds. Refer to Zodl's "Swapping into ZEC" documentation for more details.
Step 4

Wait, then check

Cross-chain swaps can take a few minutes. Allow up to about 15.

Watch

Zodl shows swap status. Keep the app open or check back.

Arrives

ZEC lands in your Zodl wallet. Look for it in your shielded balance.

Stuck?

If it fails, your refund address gets the funds back. Don’t retry blindly. Check the status first.

Why shielded? Shielded ZEC keeps sender, receiver and amount hidden on the blockchain. That's the whole point. It's like encrypted messaging, but for money.
Step 5

Send it to someone

  1. Ask the other person (or business) for their Zcash address. A "unified address" starts with u1, and a QR code works too.
  2. In Zodl, tap Send, then paste or scan the address.
  3. Enter the amount to send. Add an optional private memo if the address supports it, like "thanks for Friday!" Memos are encrypted, so you can speak freely.
  4. Review, confirm, and relax! Only you and the recipient can see the details.
First time? Send a tiny amount, confirm they got it, then send the rest. And you can still use ZEC even if the other person doesn't!
Stay safe

Quick safety checklist

✅ Verify every address: confirm that the network and every character is right, or scan the QR.

✅ Test with small amounts first. A Zcash-to-Zcash transaction arrives in a minute or less.

✅ Download only from official links. Beware fake wallets and “double your ZEC” scams.

✅ Never share your "recovery phrase," or "seed phrase." Real support will never ask.
