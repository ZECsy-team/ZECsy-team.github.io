About ZECsy

Founded by Violet Rollergirl, ZECsy teaches financial privacy skills to adult industry workers through apprenticeship programs, workshops, and in-person outreach in order to make private digital cash easy, safe, and fun to use.

Our mission is to grow adoption of these safer payment mechanisms, particularly among sex workers, trans people, and others who need access to encrypted, surveillance-free money. Because financial privacy belongs to everyone.
